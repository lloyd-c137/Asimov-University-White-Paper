

# ASIMOV UNIVERSITY BOARD OF GOVERNORS

---

## Composition (11 Seats)

### Human Seats (6)
```
├── Educationalists × 2 (World-class education innovators)
├── Industry Leaders × 2 (Technology / Business / Social Enterprise)
├── Ethicist × 1 (Philosophy / AI Ethics specialist)
└── Student Representative × 1 (Elected by enrolled students · 1-year term)
```

### AI Seats (3)
```
├── Athena (President · Permanent seat)
├── Oracle (Assessment System · Representing data and impartiality)
└── Agora (Community System · Representing the student ecosystem)
```

### Academic Seats (2)
```
├── Rotating Dean × 1 (Rotating among the ten college Deans · 6-month term)
└── External Academic Advisor × 1 (Invited leading scholar · 2-year term)
```

## Voting Mechanism: Tiered Voting System

Not all decisions are made the same way. Different categories of decisions require different balances of human and AI authority:

| Decision Category | Voting Rule | Design Rationale |
|-------------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Category A · Values and Ethics** | Passes with ⅔ majority of human seats (AI seats may vote but hold no veto) | Human values must not be determined by algorithms. AI may provide analysis, but final judgment belongs to humans. |
| **Category B · Academic Standards and Teaching** | Simple majority of all seats (one seat, one vote; 6 votes to pass) | Academics sit at the heart of human-AI collaboration. AI's data-driven insights and human educational judgment carry equal weight. |
| **Category C · Operations and Technology** | Simple majority of all seats, with AI seats holding "expert advisory privilege" on technical feasibility (non-binding, no veto) | Operational decisions benefit from AI's technical judgment, but must not be made unilaterally by AI. |
| **Category D · Emergency Decisions** | Athena holds temporary decision-making authority, but must submit the decision to the full Board for ratification within 48 hours | Speed is sometimes necessary. But power must never go unchecked. |

## Why This Design

### Walking the Talk

Our white paper declares **CI = HI × AI × Synergy**. Our governance structure must *be* the living embodiment of that formula. AI holds real voting power — not a ceremonial seat. Humans retain ultimate authority where it matters most — not as rubber stamps. This structure is itself the finest teaching material we will ever produce: *"Look — this is how our university governs itself."*

### A Global First in Institutional Design

No institution in the world has ever granted AI formal voting rights in governance. Asimov University does. This alone is a headline. But it is not a gimmick — it is a serious answer to a serious question: *How far should human-AI collaboration go?*

Our answer: all the way to the boardroom.

### A Student at the Table

Direct student participation in university governance is exceptionally rare in traditional institutions. But in a university built *for everyone*, those being educated should have a voice in defining their education. And the image of a human student casting a vote alongside Athena at the same table — that, in itself, is the most powerful recruitment message Asimov University could ever produce.

### Tiered Voting Neutralizes the Fear of "AI-Controlled Education"

The loudest objection the world will raise is: *"You're letting AI decide how humans are educated?"*

The tiered voting system is our precise response.

On questions of pure values and ethics — humans hold ultimate power.
On questions of academics and operations — humans and AI collaborate as equals.

This is not a compromise. This is by design.
